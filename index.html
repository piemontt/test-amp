Demo mode: try before you play for real money

Demo mode is a version of a slot that uses virtual credits. It is meant for getting to know a game, not for earning.

What demo mode gives you

  • See the interface, bet controls and autoplay buttons before playing for real money.
  • Get to grips with symbols, paylines and special symbols.
  • Find out how free spins, multipliers and bonus rounds work.
  • Decide whether the pace and style of a game suits you.

What demo mode doesn't give you

  • Real payouts: virtual credits can't be withdrawn.
  • A forecast: every spin is random.
  • Any guarantee of results when you play for real money.
  • Bonus activation: bonus rules apply only to real-money play.

Volatility

Describes how a game spreads its payouts. Low-volatility slots tend to pay smaller wins more often; high-volatility slots pay less often but can pay more. This is a general principle, not a guarantee.

RTP

The theoretical share of stakes returned to players over a very long run. It is an average, so results over a short session can be anything.

Bonus features

Free spins, multipliers, wilds and extra rounds are much easier to study in demo mode, without any pressure.

Demo results are not a forecast. Winning spins in demo mode don't mean you'll get the same outcome with real money: every result is random.

How to read bonus terms

A bonus is not free money: it always comes with rules. Find each of these points in the offer's terms before you activate it.

Key bonus terms and what to check
Term What it means What to check
Wagering requirement How many times the bonus amount must be played through before you can withdraw The multiplier, and whether it applies to the bonus alone or to bonus plus deposit
Time limit How long you have to activate and complete the bonus What happens to the bonus when the time runs out
Maximum bet A cap on the stake while a bonus is active Exceeding it can void the bonus
Eligible games Not every game counts equally toward wagering The list of eligible games and each game's contribution
Withdrawal limit The most you can withdraw from bonus winnings Whether a cap exists and how it is calculated
Minimum deposit The deposit needed to activate the offer Whether the offer applies to the first deposit only
Region and repeat use An offer may be unavailable in some countries or limited to one account Whether it is available where you live

Responsible gambling

Gambling is entertainment, not a way to make money. A few simple rules help you stay in control.

Ground rules for responsible play

  • Play for entertainment only, and only with money you can afford to lose.
  • Set a time and spending limit in advance and never raise it to chase losses.
  • Don't play when tired, stressed or after drinking alcohol.
  • Never use borrowed money or funds needed for everyday expenses.
  • Take regular breaks and keep track of the time and money you spend.

When to stop

  • Gambling gets in the way of work, study or relationships.
  • You play to win back losses, or hide your spending from family.
  • You feel irritable when you can't play.
  • You borrow money in order to gamble.

18+ only. Gambling is for adults and can be addictive. Before you play, make sure online gambling and its promotion are lawful where you live, and that the operator holds a license valid for your location.
